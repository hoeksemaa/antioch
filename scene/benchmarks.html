Person detection

Every detector runs on the same 100 fixed scenarios, which mix every setting. Detection rate: the share of frames with the person in view in which the detector found the person. False alarms: person boxes per minute in scenes with no person (dog, passing car, empty). Click a setting to list its scenarios, and a cell to replay it. All scenes are simulated and uncalibrated.

    Scoring notes

    • The true box covers the whole body, also the parts behind a railing, a wall, a tree, or the package. A correct box on the visible part can overlap less than 0.3; then it counts as a miss and a false alarm.
    • A person inside the camera's frame counts as in view, also when a tree hides them or most of the body is outside the frame. No detector can find them, so these frames lower every detector's rate.
    • A detector that finds few people also makes few false alarms. Read the two together.
    • The detectors learned from real photos (COCO). None of them saw this simulation before.
